Monday's gazette carried Resolution 117-2026 from the Autoridad Reguladora de Aguas de Valdora (ARAV), finding Compañía Minera Cerro Alto, S.A. liable for the degradation of the Serranía del Toldo aquifer.

Three features make the instrument consequential: it takes effect immediately upon publication, it starts a 90-day clock for the comprehensive remediation plan ordered for the aquifer, and it imposes a V$ 4.2 million fine on the company.

Containment works at the Cerro Alto tailings dam proceed under supervision, with monthly progress reporting until the remediation plan is approved. The company may seek reconsideration before the authority's board within 15 business days.
