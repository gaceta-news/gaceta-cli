Valdora's water authority on Monday found Compañía Minera Cerro Alto, S.A. liable for degrading the Serranía del Toldo aquifer in the Vega Norte sector.

Resolution 117-2026, published in the official gazette, fines the company V$ 4.2 million and orders a comprehensive remediation plan for the aquifer within 90 days, plus immediate containment works at the Cerro Alto tailings dam under the regulator's supervision, with monthly progress reporting.

The resolution takes effect immediately; Compañía Minera Cerro Alto may seek reconsideration before the authority's board within 15 business days.
